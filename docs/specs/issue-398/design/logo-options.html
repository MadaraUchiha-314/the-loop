the-loop — logo options

Five marks, five concepts of inner, outer and many loops working together. Each is one standalone SVG, hand-drawn by a generator (a seeded wobble along every loop, a pen that swells and tapers), in one muted palette. Shown on paper, on slate, at four sizes down to a favicon, and beside the name. Pick one on the ticket; feedback becomes a parameter change and a re-run.

option 1 · option-1-gimbal.svg

Gimbal

Nested loops that turn on one centre.

the-loop
  • The outer ring is the work item's loop; the two rings inside it are the loops it carries, each at its own tilt.
  • The dot is the ticket — the one fixed point every loop turns on.
  • Three ellipses, three tilts, one pivot: an armillary sphere drawn by hand.

Drawn as: three tilted ellipses and a dot, each ribbon a seeded wobble and a swelling pen; 4 ribbons + 3 sketch passes.

option 2 · option-2-one-line.svg

One line

One continuous stroke: an outer loop that dips inward to make three inner loops and returns to close.

the-loop
  • The pen never lifts — the outer loop and the inner loops are one process, not several.
  • Three inner loops meet at the centre: the pull requests, each a loop of its own, all part of the same line.
  • The dot is the ticket the line is drawn around.

Drawn as: an epitrochoid (a circle of radius 1 rolling around a circle of radius 3, tracing a point 2.15 out), one ribbon, tapered where the hand starts and stops.

option 3 · option-3-coil.svg

Coil

A loop whose line is itself made of loops.

the-loop
  • The outer loop is not a line but a coil — eighteen small loops, one continuous path: loops all the way down.
  • Inside it, one plain loop: the inner loop the outer one carries.
  • The dot is the ticket at the centre.

Drawn as: an epitrochoid with eighteen inward loops (a circle of radius 1 around one of radius 18, tracing a point 1.5 out) as one thin ribbon, plus one plain ring.

option 4 · option-4-rings.svg

Rings

Many loops overlapping; where they overlap, the colour deepens — that is where the work happens.

the-loop
  • One large loop and three smaller ones, each translucent, laid over each other like rings left on a table.
  • Every overlap makes a colour none of the loops has alone: the loops working together are visible as new colour.
  • No loop is complete without the others — the large one holds them, the small ones give it its shape.

Drawn as: four wobbly bands at 85% opacity, blended with `multiply` on paper and `screen` on slate (plain alpha where a renderer has neither).

option 5 · option-5-planetary.svg

Planetary

Loops that drive loops: an outer ring, three planets inside it, a sun at the centre — a planetary gear drawn as loops.

the-loop
  • The outer ring is the work item's loop; the three loops meshing with it are the pull requests, turning as it turns.
  • The sun in the middle is the ticket every planet also meshes with.
  • Each planet touches both the ring and the sun: no loop turns alone.

Drawn as: five circles in the proportions of a planetary gear set (ring 101, planets 27 at radius 70, sun 40).
